Welcome

Vivekananda Sansad proudly presents the 53rd Year of Sarbojanin Sri Sri Jagadhatri Puja 2026, celebrating a rich tradition of devotion, culture, and community spirit since 1974. Held from 15th to 21st November 2026 in Vivekvill, Howrah, this grand celebration brings together thousands of devotees and visitors in a vibrant atmosphere of faith, unity, and festivity.
